# 01 — Brand

## Company

**Nexor AI** is an AI automation agency for local service businesses.

We install a complete done-for-you system that captures leads, responds instantly, follows up automatically, and converts more customers — so service business owners stop losing jobs to missed calls and slow follow-ups.

**One-line pitch:** Every missed opportunity becomes a captured lead.

## Audience

Local service business owners — usually owner-operators or small teams running:

- HVAC
- Roofing
- Auto glass
- Plumbing
- Electrical
- General home services

**Their pain:** They miss calls during jobs. They respond slowly. They have no follow-up system. Their websites don't convert. They rely on referrals and inconsistent lead flow.

**Their mindset:** Practical. Skeptical of "tech." Care about results, not features. Want it to work without them learning anything new.

## Offer

- **Free** high-converting website build ($0 upfront)
- **Monthly fee** for the automation system (~$297/month)
- Optional trial offer for early clients

This is mentioned on the FAQ but is not the lead message. The hero sells outcomes, not pricing.

## Positioning

- Premium but simple
- Results-focused, not feature-focused
- Not overly technical — no jargon
- Built specifically for service businesses (not "for everyone")
- Done-for-you (we handle setup; they don't lift a finger)

## Voice & Tone

**Be:**
- Direct
- Confident
- Clear
- Minimal
- Plain-spoken — talk like a contractor would talk to another contractor

**Avoid:**
- Buzzwords: "revolutionary AI," "cutting-edge," "next-gen," "synergy," "leverage," "unlock"
- Playful or gimmicky language
- Overly technical explanations of how the AI works
- Hype, exclamation marks, urgency tricks
- Generic SaaS landing-page phrasing

**Tone test:** If a phrase would feel out of place coming from a roofer talking to another roofer, cut it.

## Conversion Goals

- **Primary:** Book a demo call
- **Secondary:** Capture lead via form (name, business, phone/email)

The "Book a Demo" CTA appears in the nav, hero, end of every major section, and final CTA block. Same exact wording every time.
